## Method

1. **Make the dough.** Crumble the fresh yeast into the bowl of a stand mixer
   fitted with a dough hook. Add the lukewarm water and stir until the yeast
   dissolves. Add the grated potato, flour and salt, then knead for about
   10 minutes into a smooth, supple dough. Cover and let it rise at room
   temperature for about 40 minutes.

2. **Shape the lángos.** Turn the dough out onto a floured surface and divide it
   into equal pieces — one per lángos. Roll each piece into an oval and lay them
   out on two baking-paper-lined trays. Cover and let them rise for a further
   10 minutes.

3. **Heat the oil** to 170–180 °C in a deep frying pan with high sides, or a wide
   saucepan.

4. **Fry in batches**, about 3 minutes per side, until puffed and golden. Lift
   each one out and drain on a rack set over kitchen paper.

5. **Make the garlic oil.** Peel and grate the garlic and stir it into the oil.

6. **Serve.** Peel and finely chop the red onion. Brush each warm lángos with the
   garlic oil, then top with sour cream, roe, red onion and dill.
